### 🎯 What is TypeCast?
Language learners often struggle with disconnected learning: memorizing word lists in isolation, listening to podcasts passively without catching details, and stumbling when trying to speak.

**TypeCast** bridges listening comprehension, muscle memory typing, and spoken communication into a single unified loop:

1. 👂 **True Acoustic Ear Training (Dictation & Blind Typing)**  
   Break free from subtitles. Train your brain to directly catch connected speech, reductions, and trailing consonants until your auditory reflexes become second nature.
2. 🧠 **In-Context Vocabulary Retention (Muscle Memory)**  
   Say goodbye to flashcard cramming. Type words letter-by-letter in authentic contexts—ordering food, police inquiries, job interviews, or concert chats.
3. 🗣️ **Authentic Everyday Spoken English (Natural Fluency)**  
   Over 375 real-world situational episodes from daily life. Typing each sentence rehearses real spoken conversations before you actually need them.

---

### ⚔️ The 4-Stage Progressive Challenge (Hardcore Unlocking)
TypeCast replaces passive browsing with a **gamified pass-to-unlock mechanism**. You cannot skip ahead without proving mastery:

```
Stage 1: [Show]   Full Text Typing      · Master spelling & acoustic rhythm
Stage 2: [Blur]   Blurred Hints         · Force the ears to identify phonetic details
Stage 3: [Hidden] Blind Dictation       · Pure listening typing with equal-width slots
Stage 4: [Final]  The Grand Challenge   · Timed test evaluating WPM & accuracy
```

> 🔒 **Passing Criteria**:  
> You must score **≥ 80 points** in the Final Challenge to unlock the next real-world episode. Sub-par attempts require retraining, ensuring true retention over superficial progress.

- **⏸️ Play/Pause Audio**: Step away anytime without auditory clutter or timer penalty.
- **🔁 Instant Tab Replay**: Press `Tab` to loop the current sentence seamlessly until fully grasped.
- **💾 Automatic Local Save**: Zero login required. All level unlocks and high scores are securely saved in your browser via IndexedDB.

---

### 📻 Curated Content & Acoustic Precision
- **Official Open RSS**: Audio directly streamed from *Slow English Podcast with Emilie*.
- **Acoustic Padding**: Each sentence features `-200ms` head padding and `+180ms` tail padding to eliminate word clipping.
- **Pure Learning Content**: Intros, outros, Patreon plugs, and promotional links are intelligently filtered out.

### 🎯 为什么叫「一举多得」？
很多英语学习者面临脱节困境：抱着单词书死记硬背、刷播客模模糊糊不知所云、开口说话吞吞吐吐。

**TypeCast 将听力、打字拼写与口语表达立体整合，打一篇文章，多维同步提升：**

1. 👂 **真·磨耳朵（精听盲听）**：摆脱字幕依赖，精准捕捉连读、弱读与辅音气流，直到耳朵形成生理级反射。
2. 🧠 **真实语境记单词（肌肉记忆）**：在点餐、问路、面试等真实场景中逐字母敲击，手眼脑耳协同，告别死记硬背。
3. 🗣️ **地道生活场景口语（脱口而出）**：全量 375 期纯正日常口语，跟打一遍就是肌肉预演，日常对话自然脱口而出。

---

### ⚔️ 四阶梯硬核闯关机制（通关解锁制）
TypeCast 引入了**严格的关卡考核机制**，必须考核合格才能解锁下一个生活场景：

```
第一阶梯：【看打（Show）】    明文识音 · 熟悉发音节奏与拼写框架
第二阶梯：【虚打（Blur）】    模糊抓词 · 文本若隐若现，强迫耳朵抓取发音细节
第三阶梯：【盲打（Hidden）】  单句盲听 · 纯听辨敲击，等宽槽位弹性自适应折行
第四阶梯：【全文大挑战（Final）】硬核大考 · 综合考察打字语速 WPM 与击键准确率
```

> 🔒 **硬核考核规则**：  
> 第四轮大挑战成绩必须达到 **≥ 80 分（合格）** 才能达成通关！系统才会为你解锁下一个生活场景，倒逼语言真正内化。

- **⏸️ 随心暂停**：临时离席一键暂停音频与计时，杜绝听觉污染与计时失真。
- **🔁 Tab 键快速重听**：生难句单键无限回听，直到彻底听清。
- **💾 进度自动保存**：无需注册账号，学习进度与关卡解锁状态实时安全保存在本地浏览器（IndexedDB），关掉网页下次打开依然继续！
